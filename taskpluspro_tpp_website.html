Help centre

Frequently asked questions

Why must I confirm my email?

Email confirmation helps verify that you control the address used for your account. Check your inbox and spam folder for the Supabase confirmation message.

Are the tasks shown here live?

No. The cards are examples for the page design. A live marketplace requires task records, application flows, moderation and secure payout handling in the backend.

Is the $5 payment active?

No. This HTML page does not process membership payments. Do not assume membership has been activated until the backend verifies a real provider transaction.

Are referral rewards credited automatically?

Not by this HTML alone. A referral code in a URL is not proof that a referral is valid or that a reward has been earned. The server must validate and record eligible referrals.
